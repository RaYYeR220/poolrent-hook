# Evidence

Local evidence record for one exact submission revision of `poolrent`. Everything here is
**builder-declared and untrusted** until a maintainer reproduces it. Local tool results are kept
separate from independent review, deployment receipts, source verification, runtime matching,
lifecycle proof, routing review and product availability — none of which exist for this revision.

| Source verification, runtime matching, lifecycle proof | **None.** These require a deployment that does not exist. |

All four executed gross quote-side cases are covered, in both the `beforeSwap` and the `afterSwap`
quadrants, with the executed amount — not the requested amount — as the basis. Rounding is
directional and declared: down on the legs carved out of a known gross amount, up on the legs
grossed onto a net amount, and up for the platform's half of each split so per-swap rounding can
never leave the immutable owner short. Owner-only claims are proven against the immutable address,
including owner-selected per-claim destinations, and against failed attempts by the builder, the
project, an administrator-like caller, the launcher, a manager and arbitrary addresses. There is no
stored mutable recipient, rescue, sweep or redirect path; storage-overwrite attempts leave the
constant owner unchanged. Accrual plus partial and full claims reconcile to the remaining liability
and the backing balance to the wei.

The quote-asset-derived before/after return-delta path is asserted per swap mode. The self-call
policy is `same-pool-swap-fee-enforced-internally`, which the standard requires from any hook that
makes direct PoolManager calls. This hook makes exactly four — `donate`, `take`, `sync` and `settle`,
all inside `afterSwap` — and never `swap` or `modifyLiquidity`, so there is no hook-initiated
same-pool swap for v4's self-call callback suppression to skip a charge on. A test proves no path
from the hook reaches `PoolManager.swap`.

## What this evidence does not prove

It does not prove that the contracts are audited, accepted, integrated, deployed, verified, routed,
supported by any provider, or available — and it does not prove that the mandatory fee is collected
live anywhere. It proves that at one exact commit, under one exact toolchain, the described
behaviour is what the code does, including against the contracts currently deployed on Ethereum
mainnet.
